SupplyBeacon

Explainable graph-based supply-chain risk intelligence

I built SupplyBeacon to connect electronics product data, supply-network relationships and disruption evidence in one explainable workflow. Instead of asking an LLM to invent an answer, the system retrieves graph and operational evidence first, then builds a grounded explanation.

The current platform combines NetworkX graph analytics, deterministic risk scoring, GraphRAG, LangGraph orchestration, a warehouse-disruption Scenario Lab, historical shipping ML and a React analytics interface. The operational risk score is a derived index — not a claimed probability of disruption.

MemoryLoop AI

Guided memory-rehearsal concept for dementia support

MemoryLoop AI explores a structured rehearsal loop for everyday plans: prepare a task, practise recalling details, review simple feedback, and revisit important categories. The public demo includes patient, caregiver, and clinician-style views with synthetic tasks, browser-side rule-based feedback, and simulated reminder stages.

The separate local development version uses FastAPI and SQLite. Its backend is not included in this public repository and is not connected to the hosted demo. The project is an educational prototype, not a clinically validated product.
